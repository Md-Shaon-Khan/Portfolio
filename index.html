Projects

HealthBridge

A full-stack clinical intelligence platform that connects biomedical hardware with AI-driven analysis. It acquires ECG signals in real time, predicts cardiac conditions and general health risks, and gives patients and doctors a shared dashboard.

  • ECG acquisition with an Arduino UNO and AD8232 sensor at 360 Hz, logged to an SD card in CSV format.
  • Signal pipeline with artifact removal, a 0.5–45 Hz band-pass filter, a 50 Hz notch filter and per-segment Z-score normalization.
  • ResNet1D model that classifies ten-second segments into 8 classes, trained on MIT-BIH, PTB-XL and CPSC 2018.
  • Risk prediction from vital signs and symptoms with Scikit-learn, returning a risk score and clinical recommendations.
  • Patient and doctor dashboards with report history, prescription feedback and an AI medical chatbot.

কৃষক বন্ধু (Farmer's Friend)

Ongoing

An AI-powered rice leaf analysis platform that helps farmers assess paddy leaves from a photograph. A two-stage deep learning pipeline first confirms that the image shows a paddy leaf, then classifies it as Bacterial, Fungal, Viral or Healthy.

  • Stage 1: a MobileNetV3-Small gatekeeper separates paddy from non-paddy images, with a 0.85 confidence threshold.
  • Stage 2: a ConvNeXt-Tiny classifier at 384 × 384 resolution predicts the pathogen type.
  • Grad-CAM-based localization marks the regions of the leaf that drive the diagnosis.
  • Image similarity search over ConvNeXt embeddings returns visually similar reference leaves.
  • Bangla treatment guidance, served through a FastAPI backend.
  • PyTorch
  • ConvNeXt-Tiny
  • MobileNetV3

LockGuard

A hardware-based smart door security system developed as a Digital Logic Design course project. It uses dual-factor authentication to control door access and raises an alert on unauthorized entry attempts.

  • Built from discrete digital logic components, without a microcontroller or software stack.
  • Access is granted only when both independent authentication factors are satisfied.
  • Combinational logic for the authentication decision and sequential logic for the lock and alert states.
  • Documented with circuit diagrams, truth tables and a full project report.

ArogyoAI · The Infinity AI BuildFest 2026

ArogyoAI is an AI-powered clinical decision support platform for preliminary screening and risk assessment, designed to assist patients and healthcare professionals in Bangladesh. It supports doctors rather than replacing them, and every output is intended for physician validation.

  • Adaptive symptom questionnaire in Bangla and English.
  • AI modules for chest X-ray (DenseNet121, ResNet50, ViT-Base ensemble), ECG (1D ResNet with Transformer) and skin disease (EfficientNet-B3), with a CT scan module planned.
  • Confidence scores, inconclusive-result detection below 60%, and an overall risk score from 0 to 100 that triggers alerts for high-risk patients.
  • Grad-CAM visual explanations and a doctor dashboard with patient prioritization, messaging and a feedback loop.
